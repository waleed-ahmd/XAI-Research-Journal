# XAI Assignment 02: Presentation

- **Group Category:** Explainable AI
- **Group Name:** Group 7

## Purpose

Communicate your group's chosen XAI topic clearly to peers within a tight time constraint.

## Requirements

**Length:** 10 minutes, strictly timed, plus a short Q&A (3-5 minutes) from me and your class peers.

**Participation:** All 4 group members should speak substantively. You may use a split is roughly 2.5 minutes each, or you can allocate by strength (e.g., one member handles Q&A lead) as long as it's not one person presenting while others stand silently.

**Content expectations:** topic/motivation, the core technical content (method(s), how they work, what they explain and how), a critical angle (limitations, open questions, or a critique. Avoid just doing a summary), and connection back to course concepts, when and where applicable.

**Slides** required, but the presentation is graded on delivery and content, not slide polish alone.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content accuracy & depth | 35% | Technically correct, appropriately deep for a 10-minute format |
| Structure & clarity | 20% | Logical flow, a clear throughline, non-experts in the audience can follow it |
| Critical framing | 15% | Goes beyond summary — limitations, open questions, or a genuine critique are present |
| Delivery & time management | 15% | Confident delivery, finishes within the time limit, all members participate meaningfully |
| Handling Q&A | 10% | Responds accurately; "I don't know, but here's my best guess" is an acceptable and honest answer. Obvious bluffing will be penalized, not uncertainty. |
| Visual design | 5% | Slides support rather than duplicate the spoken content; readable, not text-dense |
