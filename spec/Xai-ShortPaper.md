# XAI Assignment 03: Short Paper

- **Group Category:** Explainable AI
- **Group Name:** Group 7

## Purpose

A focused, technically grounded treatment of an XAI topic of the group's choosing, written to professional conference-paper standards.

## Requirements

**Length:** Maximum 4 pages, including references (references must be on their own page).

**Format:** IEEE conference two-column format.

- Official templates (Word and LaTeX): https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html
- Easiest LaTeX option, no install required: search "IEEE conference template" on Overleaf — it hosts an official, ready-to-edit version.
- Standard conventions apply: Times New Roman, 10pt body text in the template's two-column layout, no manually adjusted margins.

**Structure** (typical IEEE conference sections): Abstract, Introduction, Background/Related Work, Methodology or Technical Approach, Results/Analysis or Discussion, Conclusion & Limitations, References.

**Topic scope:** A specific XAI method, technique comparison, application domain, or critique — specific enough to go deep in 4 pages. "Explainable AI" as a whole is too broad; "a comparison of SHAP and LIME on tabular credit-scoring data" or "a critique of chain-of-thought faithfulness in LLM interpretability" are the right scale.

**Citations:** IEEE numbered citation style; expect a mix of the course's assigned readings and additional sources the group finds themselves.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | What I will look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical accuracy & depth | 30% | Correct, sufficiently deep treatment of the chosen topic; not a surface-level summary |
| Critical analysis | 20% | Genuine analysis or critique, not just a literature summary. Your group's own reasoning should be visible |
| Structure & clarity of argument | 20% | Follows a clear IEEE-style structure; each section does its job; the abstract accurately previews the paper |
| Adherence to format | 10% | Correct IEEE two-column template, within the 4-page limit (excluding references), correct citation style, cites uses effectively and meaningfully. |
| Quality & use of sources | 10% | Sources are appropriate and correctly attributed; claims are actually supported by what's cited (not just present in the reference list) |
| Conclusion & limitations | 10% | Explicitly states what the group's analysis does *not* cover or resolve — self-awareness about scope is rewarded, not penalized |
