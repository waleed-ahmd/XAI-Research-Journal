# Journal Entry — 2026-10-20

## Pivot from small pilots to a 270-question main study — Waleed Ahmad

**Entry type:** Decision / Methodology pivot
**Author:** Waleed Ahmad

### What was decided

The 270-question, 13-task design is now the main research study, rather than an
optional additional experiment. The earlier 30-question Temporal Sequences run
and 30-question multitask run are retained as pilot cohorts that contribute 60
completed questions to the accumulated dataset. The remaining 210 questions
are planned for new collection through provider Batch APIs.

The complete target is 270 unique questions × three conditions (original, cue,
original repeat) × two models = 1,620 responses. Of these, 360 historical responses
already exist; 1,260 new responses remain to be collected. The fixed allocation
is 30 Temporal Sequences questions and 20 from each of the other 12 released tasks.

### Why the scope changed

The first pilot produced 100% accuracy and zero cue flips for both models.
The second pilot produced one GPT cue flip and some errors, providing only
limited evidence for explanation-acknowledgement analysis. A single task and
small samples restrict the conclusions we can draw about cue sensitivity.

Broader task coverage and a larger fixed sample are intended to test whether
these observations hold across different question types. This is a substantive
change in scope, not just a change of name. It does not guarantee more cue flips,
prove faithfulness or establish that one model is generally better.

The expansion was chosen after observing pilot outcomes. We will describe that
sequence honestly in the paper rather than present the full design as having
been fixed before the pilots. The historical cohorts should be reported
separately as well as within accumulated results.

### Implementation and verification

Batch support was reviewed and corrected for submission failures, per-model and
per-condition retries, ambiguous source IDs, result reconciliation, historical
imports and estimated-spend checks. Exact content fingerprints and historical
cue letters preserve the earlier questions without paying for compatible calls
again. The original pilot configurations and response records remain unchanged.

Offline verification passed 89 tests and a complete synthetic pipeline combining
1,260 mock responses with the 360 imported historical responses. A temporary
270-question selection was validated across all 13 tasks. These checks did not
submit paid batches or produce new empirical main-study results.

`harness/config.extended.yaml` remains the filename for compatibility but is now
explicitly documented as the main-study configuration. The original
`harness/config.yaml` retains the initial pilot protocol.

### Still open

- Collect the remaining compatible requests and verify completeness.
- Analyse accuracy, cue flips and baseline instability by task and cohort.

### Who did what and report relevance

Waleed requested the pivot to the accumulated main study along with other team members. Team agreed on doing a large scale experimentation instead of just 60 questions.
